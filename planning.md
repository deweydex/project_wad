# Planning

Complete this **before** you write any HTML or CSS. It is worth 20 of the
project's 70 marks on its own, and it makes the building phase faster:
you will know what you are building and why, instead of deciding as you
go.

Answer every question in your own words. A short, specific answer is
worth more than a long vague one.

## 1. Purpose and audience
